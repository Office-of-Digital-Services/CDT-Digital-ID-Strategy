Mobile driver’s license (mDL)

Projects
  • Projects
  • Mobile driver’s license (mDL)

The California Department of Technology (CDT) is excited to announce AccessCA (formerly California Identity Gateway) now supports reading California mobile driver's licenses (mDLs) through the CA DMV Wallet app. Beginning October 1, state agencies using AccessCA can now accept and verify mDLs, which resident can share directly from their phone.

Why mDLs

Mobile driver's licenses are increasingly becoming a preferred way for people to prove who they are. More than 4.2 million California residents have joined the Department of Motor Vehicle’s (DMV) mDL pilot program, where they can add their state-issued ID to the digital wallet on their phone.

AccessCA's new mDL feature prepares state services to accept this growing technology, while empowering residents with direct control over the Personally Identifiable Information (PII) they share.

Key Highlights & Capabilities

  • mDL Support via CA DMV Wallet: The Identity Gateway will support reading mDLs from the CA DMV Wallet application, with future expansion planned for native device wallets (Apple Wallet, Google Wallet, Samsung Wallet) and mobile passports.
  • Privacy-First Architecture: By leveraging mDLs, the Gateway enables selective disclosure—allowing individuals to share only the minimum data required for a specific service, placing data ownership and control directly in the hands of Californians.
  • Expanded Addressable Population: Integrating mDLs provides an additional, highly accessible verification pathway, driving statewide adoption by making digital credentials immediately useful for accessing government benefits and services.
  • Launch of the AskCA GenAI Chatbot: Deploying simultaneously on October 1, 2026, the AskCA GenAI chatbot will be the inaugural application to utilize this new functionality, providing residents with two secure identity verification pathways directly through conversational AI interactions.

Next Steps & Action Items

State entities planning digital service modernizations or looking to integrate robust, privacy-preserving identity verification should review their enterprise architectures to align with the October 1, 2026 availability.

For technical guidance, onboarding support, or further details on integrating with the California Identity Gateway, please contact the California Department of Technology or visit cdt.ca.gov.
